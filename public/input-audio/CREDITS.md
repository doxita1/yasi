# Input Lab audio

The four listening passages and 60 academic dictation clips were synthesized for this site from original scripts and lexical examples. Piper produced the educational narration with the **en_GB-alan-medium** and **en_US-lessac-medium** voice models. Alan voices the British lecturer and one student. Lessac voices the American guide and the other student. The models alternate across the advanced word clips. The 936 existing course pronunciations use Windows' Microsoft Zira voice and remain in the separate course audio directory.

- [Alan model card and dataset credit](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_GB/alan/medium/MODEL_CARD) — the Alan dataset is attributed to the Mycroft mimic3 **apope_low** voice.
- [Lessac model card and dataset credit](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/lessac/medium/MODEL_CARD) — Lessac Blizzard Challenge 2013 speech corpus, with linked dataset terms.
- [Piper synthesis engine](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) — original engine software, installed outside this repository for audio generation.

The lesson recordings are synthetic educational examples. The actual IELTS Listening test features a wider range of speakers and accents.
